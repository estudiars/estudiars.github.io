The general formula for binomial expansion is given by (x + y)^n = summation from r = 0 to r = n of nCr (x)^(n-r) (y)^r. nCr gives the coefficient of the terms of the binomial expansion. The pascal triangle can be used in place of nCr to obtain the coefficient of the terms of the binomial expansion.

To expand a binomial, we continually add up the substitution of values into the general formula starting from when r = 0 to when r = n.
